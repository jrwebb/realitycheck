Methodology
This dashboard is an editorial interpretation of publicly available data. The "direction of travel" assessments are based on the most recent comparable data points over each time window. Reasonable people can disagree on weighting, metric selection, and what constitutes "better." That's the point — the methodology should be debatable, not invisible.
