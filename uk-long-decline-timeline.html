The Two-Track Country

The economy technically recovered from 2008. GDP returned to pre-crisis levels by 2013. But the decision to restore public finances through spending cuts rather than growth meant the things government buys with GDP — health, education, infrastructure, courts — never recovered with it. The gap between economic output and public service quality is arguably the defining feature of modern Britain.
