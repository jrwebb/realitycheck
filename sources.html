Sources

Every data source used in the dashboards, listed by domain. All publicly available. All linked. If a link breaks or a dataset is superseded, please let us know.

Health (NHS)

Waiting list volumes, referral-to-treatment performance, A&E targets, and treatment throughput.

  • NHS England — Consultant-Led Referral to Treatment Waiting Times
    Monthly statistical series. The primary source for waiting list size and 18-week RTT performance.
    Monthly
  • BMA — NHS Backlog Data Analysis
    Analysis of waiting list trends, treatment volumes, and workforce pressures. Useful for contextualising the raw NHS England figures.
    Regularly updated
  • House of Commons Library — NHS Key Statistics: England (CBP-7281)
    Comprehensive briefing covering waiting times, workforce, funding, and performance against key targets.
    Periodically updated
  • Health Foundation
    Independent analysis of NHS performance and health policy, including waiting times assessments and government progress tracking.
    Various publications

Living Standards

Real wages, nominal earnings growth, and the impact of inflation on purchasing power.

  • ONS — EARN01: Average Weekly Earnings
    Seasonally adjusted average weekly earnings by sector. The main time series for tracking nominal wage growth.
    Monthly
  • ONS — Average Weekly Earnings Time Series
    Extended time series data. Used in conjunction with CPIH (series L522) for real-terms wage calculations.
    Monthly

Safety

Overall crime rates, police recorded crime, homicide trends, and fraud.

  • ONS — Crime in England and Wales
    The Crime Survey for England and Wales (CSEW) annual bulletin. The most comprehensive measure of crime trends, combining survey data with police recorded figures.
    Annual
  • ONS — Crime and Justice Portal
    Hub for all ONS crime and justice datasets including police recorded crime, fraud, and homicide statistics.
    Various schedules

Housing

Affordability ratios, house prices, rental costs, and housing supply.

  • ONS — House Price to Workplace-Based Earnings Ratio
    Median and lower-quartile affordability ratios. The core measure of whether housing is becoming more or less affordable relative to local wages.
    Annual
  • ONS — UK House Price Index
    Monthly house price data based on HM Land Registry records. Used for tracking price trends and regional variation.
    Monthly
  • House of Commons Library — Economic Indicators (CBP-9040)
    Broader economic context including housing starts, completions, and construction sector data.
    Periodically updated

Education

Attainment at key stages, disadvantage gaps, and regional disparities.

  • DfE — Explore Education Statistics
    The Department for Education's data platform. Primary source for KS2 and KS4 attainment, school-level performance, and pupil characteristics.
    Annual
  • IfG — Performance Tracker 2025: Schools
    Assessment of school system performance including attainment, teacher recruitment, and funding trends.
    Annual (November)
  • Education Policy Institute — Publications
    Independent research on education policy, particularly the disadvantage attainment gap and regional variation.
    Various publications

Public Services

Composite assessment across courts, prisons, social care, local government, and police.

  • IfG/Nuffield Foundation — Performance Tracker 2025: Summary
    The most recent cross-service assessment. Covers hospitals, GP, adult social care, children's social care, schools, police, criminal courts, prisons, and homelessness services.
    November 2025
  • IfG/CIPFA — Performance Tracker 2023
    Previous edition. Useful for tracking year-on-year change in service quality assessments.
    January 2024
  • IfG — Performance Tracker Series
    Landing page for the full annual series, published since 2017. The primary external source for our public services composite assessment.
    Annual since 2017

Employment

Employment rate, economic inactivity, vacancies, and labour market composition.

  • ONS — Labour Force Survey / Labour Market Overview
    The primary source for employment rate, unemployment, and economic inactivity. Includes breakdowns by age, region, and reason for inactivity.
    Monthly
  • House of Commons Library — Economic Indicators (CBP-9040)
    Broader economic context including PAYE Real Time Information estimates and vacancy data.
    Periodically updated

Wellbeing

Self-reported health, life satisfaction, anxiety, and healthy life expectancy.

  • ONS — UK Measures of National Well-being
    Quarterly headline well-being measures including life satisfaction, feeling things in life are worthwhile, happiness, and anxiety.
    Quarterly
  • ONS — Health and Life Expectancies
    Healthy life expectancy and disability-free life expectancy data. Key indicator of whether longer lives are also healthier lives.
    Annual

Economy

GDP per head, productivity, and quarterly growth — not aggregate GDP.

  • ONS — GDP per Head Time Series (IHXW)
    Real GDP per capita. We use this rather than headline GDP because aggregate growth without population adjustment overstates the lived experience of economic progress.
    Quarterly
  • ONS — Gross Domestic Product Portal
    Hub for all ONS GDP datasets including first quarterly estimates, quarterly national accounts, and sector breakdowns.
    Quarterly

Cross-Cutting Sources

Sources that inform multiple domains or the overall editorial framing.

  • Institute for Government
    Independent think tank. Their Performance Tracker is the backbone of our public services assessment, and their broader policy analysis informs several other domains.
    ifg.org.uk
  • Office for National Statistics
    The UK's national statistical institute. Primary data source for six of our nine domains.
    ons.gov.uk
  • House of Commons Library
    Parliament's in-house research service. Their briefing papers synthesise data from multiple official sources into accessible analysis.
    commonslibrary.parliament.uk

A note on data freshness. We link to landing pages and series portals rather than individual releases wherever possible, so the links remain valid as new data is published. Where a specific bulletin or edition is referenced (e.g. "Performance Tracker 2025"), we link directly to that publication. If you find a broken link, the underlying data is almost certainly still available — ONS, NHS England, and the IfG rarely remove data; they restructure their websites. Try searching the organisation's site directly.
