Domain by domain

Where the country stands right now, based on the most recently available data.

Health (NHS)

Declining
7.3m on the waiting list

Down from the 7.7m peak but still triple the 2012 level. The 18-week standard hasn't been met since 2016. A&E performance remains well below target.

Living Standards

Stagnating
Real wages barely at 2008 levels

Nominal wages growing but real growth has been anaemic. A lost decade and a half, with the 2021–23 inflation shock wiping out years of modest gains.

Safety

Improving
Crime at long-run lows

The quiet success story. Violent crime and burglary on long-run downward trends. Homicides near multi-decade lows. But fraud has surged and policing is stretched.

Housing

Declining
8.2× price-to-earnings ratio

The affordability ratio has risen almost continuously. A brief crash-era dip was quickly reversed. Under-35 home ownership has collapsed.

Education

Stagnating
Disadvantage gap widened post-Covid

A decade of genuine progress closing the attainment gap was substantially reversed by the pandemic. London continues to pull ahead of everywhere else.

Public Services

Declining
Almost every service worse than 2010

Courts, prisons, social care, local government — all in crisis according to the IfG. Most were already declining before the pandemic accelerated the collapse.

Employment

Stagnating
75.0% employment rate

Headline resilience masks rising economic inactivity from long-term sickness — a new structural challenge. More work is precarious or underemployed.

Wellbeing

Declining
Self-reported health at record lows

Healthy life expectancy at its lowest since records began. Life satisfaction hasn't recovered to pre-pandemic levels. Mental health provision is overwhelmed.

Economy

Stagnating
GDP per head barely above 2008

Headline GDP recovered but per-capita growth has been anaemic. Productivity — output per hour — is the UK's defining economic failure, barely growing since the crash.
